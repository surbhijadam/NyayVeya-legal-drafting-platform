Reliable Legal Drafting. Clear Documentation.

OUR COMMITMENT

Our Mission

Access to reliable legal documentation in India should not be constrained by cost, geographic location, or complex procedural barriers. NyayaDraft empowers individuals, professionals, and small businesses to generate properly formatted, statutory legal drafts quickly, affordably, and with complete confidence.

We provide standard legal document templates that have been structured according to Indian statutory requirements and judicial precedents. Users follow an intuitive guided questionnaire, preview the completed draft in real time, and download editable Word and print-ready PDF formats.

How NyayaDraft Operates

Statutory Templates

Pre-engineered templates covering statutory legal notices, agreements, affidavits, and formal applications.

Guided Validation

Step-by-step questionnaires that check statutory dates, limitation periods, and mandatory particulars.

Instant Dual Downloads

Download print-ready PDF for immediate postal dispatch and editable Microsoft Word (.docx) for legal customization.

Permanent Access

Your drafted notices and orders remain accessible in your dashboard for future reference and re-download.

Platform Transparency: What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not provide legal advice. Our templates are standard procedural formats, not personalised legal counsel or strategic case evaluation.
  • We do not replace advocates. For contested disputes, complex contracts, or court litigation, always consult an independent enrolled advocate.
  • We do not guarantee legal outcomes. A properly drafted demand notice is a mandatory procedural step under statutes such as the NI Act, but legal determinations rest with judicial forums.
