About LegalDraft India

Making legal document drafting accessible, affordable, and simple.

Our Mission

Access to legal documentation should not be limited by geography, cost, or complexity. LegalDraft India helps individuals and small businesses generate properly formatted legal documents quickly and affordably.

We provide standard legal document templates that have been reviewed by qualified Indian advocates. Users fill in a guided form, preview the generated draft, and download it in editable Word or PDF format.

What We Do

Standard Templates

Pre-reviewed legal document templates for common needs such as legal notices, agreements, and affidavits.

Guided Forms

Step-by-step forms that ask the right questions and validate your input before generating the document.

Preview & Download

Review your completed draft before paying. Download in Word format for further editing or in PDF for printing.

Secure & Private

Your personal and legal information is kept secure. Documents are accessible only through your account.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not provide legal advice. Our templates are standard formats, not personalised legal opinions.
  • We do not replace advocates. For disputed matters, court proceedings, or complex legal questions, consult a qualified advocate.
  • We do not guarantee legal outcomes. A properly drafted document is a necessary step, but outcomes depend on many factors.
  • We do not file documents on your behalf. You are responsible for sending, filing, or executing the generated document.
